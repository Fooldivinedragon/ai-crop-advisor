About the project

Objectives

  • Recommend suitable crops using only soil test values, location and planting date
  • Fetch local historical weather automatically instead of asking the farmer for it
  • Respect the cropping season and the local climate when ranking crops
  • Estimate yield in quintals per acre with a range from past seasons
  • Give a fertilizer plan in products, bags and dates
  • Explain the whole report simply, in nine Indian languages

Limitations

  • Yield comes from a rule-based model built on typical crop requirements, not from measured farm yields.
  • The soil values of the 100 wheat training rows were generated from typical ranges; their weather is real.
  • Soil ratings compare values with each crop's needs in the dataset, not with laboratory soil-test standards.
  • Fertilizer doses are general national (ICAR) figures, not state-specific recommendations.
  • The public dataset covers temperate regions such as Kashmir poorly.
Disclaimer: this is a student decision-support prototype. Recommendations and doses are estimates – always confirm them with your local Krishi Vigyan Kendra or agriculture officer before acting on them.
