The problem

Most crop recommendation tools ask the farmer for temperature, humidity and rainfall – numbers nobody knows before the season starts – and then reply with a single crop name in English. A farmer needs more than that.

How it works

The farmer gives only what they know. Everything else is worked out automatically.

Enter soil and place

N, P, K and pH from the Soil Health Card, a district or GPS, and the sowing date.

Season and weather

Kharif, Rabi or Zaid from the date; five years of local weather from NASA POWER.

Best crops

A Random Forest model ranks 23 crops, filtered by season and checked against the local climate.

Yield and fertilizer

Expected harvest in quintals per acre, soil status, and DAP, urea and MOP with dates.
