Is it overfitting?

No. Training accuracy is {{ '%.0f' % (of.train_acc * 100) }}% and test accuracy {{ '%.2f' % (of.test_acc * 100) }}% – a gap of only {{ '%.2f' % ((of.train_acc - of.test_acc) * 100) }} points. Validation accuracy rises with more data and levels off close to the training score.

Why the score is so high

Crops in the public dataset sit in fairly narrow, well-separated ranges, and the wheat soil values were generated from typical ranges. The model fits this data very well, but on real farms, where soil and weather vary more, accuracy is expected to be lower.

That is why the system does not rely on the model alone: season rules and a climate suitability check are applied before any crop is shown.
